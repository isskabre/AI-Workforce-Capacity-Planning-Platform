\# AI Workforce Capacity Planning Platform



\## Business Problem



Operations teams often react to workload after demand has already arrived. This can lead to late staffing decisions, capacity shortages, inefficient labor allocation, and avoidable overtime.



The AI Workforce Capacity Planning Platform is designed to support proactive operational planning by forecasting future workload, estimating available workforce capacity, identifying projected capacity gaps, and generating recommendations for overtime or staffing adjustments.



\## Business Objectives



The platform will:



\- Forecast future operational workload.

\- Estimate available labor capacity.

\- Identify projected labor shortages and excess capacity.

\- Recommend when overtime may be required.

\- Support operational decision-making with explainable predictions.

\- Provide an AI assistant for natural-language questions and planning summaries.



\## Engineering Objectives



The project is designed as an enterprise-grade data and AI platform demonstrating:



\- Metadata-driven ingestion

\- Provider-independent dataset acquisition

\- Amazon S3 data lake architecture

\- Landing, Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers

\- Data quality and validation

\- Data lineage and acquisition manifests

\- Feature engineering

\- Forecasting and model evaluation

\- MLOps and model lifecycle management

\- AI-assisted operational decision support



\## Initial Dataset



The initial dataset is the DataCo SMART Supply Chain dataset.



The first acquisition provider is Kaggle. The architecture is designed to support future providers such as:



\- SharePoint

\- Amazon S3

\- REST APIs

\- FTP or SFTP

\- Enterprise databases
